MagicConverter Windows installer distribution

MagicConverter and its original artwork are licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
The license is included in the application archive and corresponding source.

Filee is credited in licenses/Filee-MIT.txt. The blank HWPX template is from
python-hwpx and carries its Apache-2.0 LICENSE, NOTICE and source reference
under magic_converter/data/hwpx.

Setup includes the pinned Windows embeddable distribution of CPython, pip,
and the Python runtime wheels listed in magic_converter-runtime.txt.
Python's license is installed in runtime/LICENSE.txt. Wheel license files
remain in each installed distribution's metadata. The source manifest
records the hashes of the original embedded archives.

Qt for Python and Qt are available under module-specific terms:
https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/licenses.html
https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licensing.html
PyMuPDF and MuPDF are offered under AGPL-3.0 or commercial terms:
https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/about.html#license-and-copyright
The pillow-heif 1.8.1 Windows wheel lists libheif 1.23.6, libde265 1.1.3,
and x265 4.3 in LICENSES_bundled.txt. The wheel's notices also describe
the compiler runtime terms. See the installed pillow_heif distribution metadata.

The engine archive contains Gyan FFmpeg/FFprobe 9.0.2 (GPL-3.0-or-later),
rhwp 0.8.7 (MIT), and its app-local Microsoft Visual C++ runtime. Original
notices are installed under app/vendor/ffmpeg and app/vendor/rhwp.
FFmpeg source revision: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/946fcce07b6dcd0331c8cc609192aeff5e1924f8
rhwp source: https://github.com/edwardkim/rhwp/tree/v0.8.7
